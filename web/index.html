ACR TI-RADS Nodule Calculator

2017 ACR ultrasound feature scoring and maximum-diameter management thresholds for adult thyroid nodules. Manually enter findings from an ultrasound interpretation.

Ultrasound characteristics

Educational/clinical reference tool only. Not diagnostic software, an ultrasound image classifier, or a substitute for individual clinical assessment. Do not enter patient identifiers.

Use largest sonographic diameter, not mean diameter.

Cystic or spongiform nodules score 0 (TR1); other feature categories are not scored.

Echogenic foci (select all that apply)

Leave all unchecked for none or large comet-tail artifacts.

Assessment

Enter ultrasound findings and select Calculate TI-RADS to display the point total, category, and size-based recommendation.

Results depend on accurate sonographic descriptors. Clinical risk factors, lymph nodes, symptoms, prior biopsy, and individual circumstances may change management. Size thresholds do not imply an emergency diagnosis.

Reference: Tessler FN et al. ACR TI-RADS Committee white paper, J Am Coll Radiol. 2017;14:587–595. DOI 10.1016/j.jacr.2017.01.046. Calculation happens entirely in your browser; no findings are sent to a server.
